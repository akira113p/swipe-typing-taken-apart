# Word list licence

`words.txt` holds the 10,000 most frequent English words with their counts, taken from
*FrequencyWords* by Hermit Dave (https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords), file
`content/2018/en/en_50k.txt`, computed from the OPUS OpenSubtitles 2018 corpus.

Changes: kept lowercase a-z words only, kept "a" and "i" as the only one-letter words,
removed offensive words, and kept the top 10,000. We ship word counts only, no subtitle text.
